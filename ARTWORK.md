# Original artwork and image prompts

All 18 raster images in `assets/` were generated for this edition with the built-in image-generation tool, then converted to lightweight WebP for use in the game. They depict fictional people, objects and locations. The supplied reference photographs guided the analogue dossier styling; they are not included or used as game assets.

Exact game evidence is always rendered as accessible HTML. Generated handwriting, clock hands, decorative detail, and architectural details do not introduce additional clues. The pocket watch is an inventory image, not a time clue. No external image service is needed at runtime.

No lettering, no logos, no watermarks, no words; the game overlays its own title. Main building centered with room for text in the dark lower quarter. High quality photography, not illustration.
